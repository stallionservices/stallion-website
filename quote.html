Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED

How long does a detail take? ▾
It depends on the service and vehicle size, but most standard details take 1.5–3 hours. A full detail on an SUV can take 3–5 hours. Luke will give you an estimated timeframe when he sends your quote.
Do you need water or power at my place? ▾
Yes — Luke will need access to a water tap and a power outlet at your property. A standard garden hose connection and a single power point is all that's needed. Just let him know when booking if access might be tricky.
What's the difference between an exterior and a full detail? ▾
An exterior detail covers the outside only — wash, wheels, tyres, windows, and trim. A full detail adds a complete interior clean: vacuum, wipe-down, dashboard, seats, door cards, and floor mats. A full detail is recommended if the car hasn't been professionally cleaned in a while.
How does the same-day pricing work? ▾
Fill in the form and Luke will text you a custom price the same day — usually within a few hours. Prices are based on your car's size, condition, and the service you choose. No hidden fees, no obligation to book.
Do I need to be home while you're detailing? ▾
Not at all — many customers are at work while Luke details their car. As long as you can get him access to the vehicle beforehand, he'll handle the rest and let you know when it's done.
What areas of Perth do you service? ▾
Stallion Services covers Perth metro, North of the River (NOR), and surrounding suburbs. If you're unsure whether we come to your area, just fill in the form and Luke will confirm.
What if it rains on the day? ▾
If the weather makes it impossible to detail your car properly, Luke will contact you to reschedule at no extra cost. Perth weather is usually on our side — but we'll always make sure the job is done right.
My car is really dirty. Will you still take it on? ▾
Absolutely — that's what we're here for. Whether it's pet hair, heavy stains, mud, or a car that hasn't been cleaned in years, Luke has seen it all. Just be upfront in the form about the condition and he'll price accordingly.
Is there a deposit required to book? ▾
For most jobs, no deposit is required — payment is made on the day once the job is complete. For jobs over $1,000, a 25% deposit is required to secure your booking.
Can you do multiple cars in one visit? ▾
Yes! Many customers book two or more cars at once. Just mention it in the form and Luke will factor it into your quote.
