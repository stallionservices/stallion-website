POLICIES

Everything you need to know about bookings, cancellations & rescheduling.

✎ CANCELLATION POLICY

We require a minimum of 24 hours notice for all cancellations. If we do not receive notice before your scheduled appointment, a $50 cancellation fee will apply to cover our time and travel costs.

We understand emergencies happen — please reach out as soon as possible and we'll do our best to work with you.

📅 RESCHEDULING POLICY

We require at least 24 hours notice to reschedule your appointment at no charge. If a reschedule request is made without sufficient notice, a $50 fee will apply.

Rescheduled bookings are subject to availability. We'll always do our best to find a time that works for you.

⛈ WET WEATHER POLICY

As a mobile detailing service, some services are weather-dependent. In the event of heavy rain or unsuitable conditions:

  • We will contact you as early as possible to discuss rescheduling at no cost to you.
  • Interior-only services can typically proceed regardless of weather.
  • If we are unable to complete a service due to weather, no charge will be applied.

💳 PAYMENT POLICY

  • Payment is due upon completion of the service.
  • We accept bank transfer, card payment, and cash.
  • For services $800 and above (such as ceramic coatings and paint correction), a 25% holding deposit is required to secure your booking. This deposit is deducted from the total cost once the job is complete.

HAVE A QUESTION?

If you're unsure about anything or need to discuss your booking, we're always happy to help.

FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I need paint correction before ceramic coating?

Yes — ceramic coating seals in whatever is underneath it. If your paint has swirl marks or scratches, they'll be locked in permanently. We always recommend at least a polish before coating to ensure the best result.

How long does ceramic coating last?

With proper maintenance, a professional ceramic coating typically lasts 2–5 years depending on the product used and how the vehicle is looked after. We recommend reapplying every 3–5 years for best results.

Why does it take 24 hours?

After the coating is applied, it needs time to cure and bond properly to your paint. During this period your car should be kept dry and away from contaminants. Rushing the cure compromises the coating's durability.

Can ceramic coating be applied to any car?

Yes — ceramic coating can be applied to virtually any vehicle. The condition of your paint determines how much prep work is needed beforehand. We'll assess your vehicle and recommend the right package.

Will it protect against rock chips or deep scratches?

Ceramic coating adds a layer of hardness to your paint but is not designed to prevent rock chips or deep scratches. It does however significantly reduce light surface scratches and swirl marks from washing.
